About Me

My name is Moeketsi Isaac Rooi Motloung, an aspiring Web Designer and Developer based in George, Western Cape. I am currently pursuing a qualification in Tourism Management at Nelson Mandela University, where I have developed a strong foundation in both academic theory and practical application.

I am passionate about leveraging creativity and technology to design meaningful and user-focused digital experiences. I am eager to contribute to projects that allow me to merge my technical abilities with my interest in innovation and design.
